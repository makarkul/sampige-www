Who We Are

Pioneering India's Semiconductor Future

Sampige Semiconductors is a fabless semiconductor company designing chipsets that reduce dependence on imported silicon. Built in India for the world, we focus on consumer electronics, IoT infrastructure, and AI — markets with massive global demand and where India has a strategic edge.


Named after the Sampige (Magnolia Champaca) — a flower symbolizing growth and elegance, native to Karnataka. Our founding team brings deep domain expertise, with a track record that includes pioneering Software Defined Radio, Direct-to-Mobile broadcast, and rural broadband connectivity.
